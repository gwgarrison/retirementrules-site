Retirement Rules

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Short version: Retirement Rules does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Retirement Rules has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code. The app does not use the network to send or receive your information.

Information stored on your device

To pre-fill calculators and show your history, the app stores the following locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage:

  • Profile details you choose to enter, such as age, income, savings, and retirement goals
  • Results of calculations you run
  • App preferences, such as the display theme

This information never leaves your device through the app. If you use iCloud or device backups, it may be included in those backups, which are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Clear Saved Data in the app to erase your profile and all saved results. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device.

Tracking

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Links to other websites

The app links to a few outside resources, such as the Social Security Administration and IRS websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies; we don't control them and receive no information from your visits.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
