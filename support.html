Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. The app has no accounts and sends nothing over the internet. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I erase everything I've entered?
Open Settings in the app and tap Clear Saved Data. This removes your profile and all saved results.

Is my data synced between my iPhone and iPad?
No. Each device keeps its own data.

Why don't my numbers match my financial advisor's?
The calculators use general rules of thumb (the 4% Rule, the 25X Rule, Fidelity's age benchmarks, and so on) and simplified assumptions about returns and inflation. Tap the info button on any calculator to see its assumptions.

Where do the Social Security figures come from?
The estimate uses the Social Security Administration's published bend-point formula and the latest Trustees Report projections, or the benefit from your own SSA statement if you enter it. For your official estimate, visit my Social Security.
